Whispers of Orpheus

October 12.2025

As i stood hollow by the gate
I watched my only love fade


Dissolving mist devoured her
The diamond eyes grew dim with blur


Their spark demolished by the dark
It left my heart with death's own mark


Her cherry lips grew as cold as stone
Drawn to the madness of Hades’throne


Once laughter danced upon them
Now only screams creep there


My vivid harp no longer plays
Without her voice to light my days


Her song is my one salvation
My sweetest cruel temptation


With her gone my world is cold and bare
No living note remains to share


She walked behind me, soft, unseen,
A fragile hope that might have been


But doubt destroyed what love has won
I turned and lost my only one


How foolish was I to turn
And leave my only love to burn


I watch her fall to the darkness' grip
I will follow, and never slip


Like i fell for her before
I will fall again once more


I will let the dim claim me whole
Taking my name, consuming my soul


My name is Orpheus and I came alone
To claim again my dearest love
