How to kill time?

October 12.2025

When there's an endless, boring day
I look for something to keep me sane
Something to take my heavy thoughts away
And cure my tired overloaded brain.


Time feels stuck, yet something slips away
Scroll through posts and shuttle playlists
Each second empties, my mind's space
Every face blurs and becomes nameless


We are all strangers in our own struggle.
Chasing stars that vanish in the sky
Ambition fading like a quiet mumble
Still time goes like an never-ending lie


Count the seconds or they are lost
Never has it been enough and might leave us all behind
Turn yourself into a machine but at what cost
Time was only made to control the human's mind


So I wonder what it's like to kill time
To drift away into the unknown
Break the pattern of the clock's crime
And watch the world turn into stone
