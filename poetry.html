Poetry

June 10.2026

Poetry is such a beautiful tool
It squeeze everything from my soul
It shows the deepest mind pool
Embracing my disparity in whole


Tears are vivid, screams are cheerful
But when i put the pen on the table
Everything blurs out the fearful
Makes my moving unable


The ink and pen are my dear friends
Them comfort my distress
And welcome broken ends
Without even a slight press
