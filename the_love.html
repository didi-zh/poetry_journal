Love

June 10.2026

People say you will know when it comes.
You will feel it.
The love of the person of your dreams.
That soulmates exist, that there is one for everyone.


But what are those sorts of clichés?


The man of your dream should be this
The woman of your dream should be that
Dont do that, love should be this


What are those rules? What are those walls?
When we cannot answer the simplest question: What even is love?

Why do people put it in boxes,
trying to tick every requirement and call it love?
Why does love need to be defined?
The beauty of the feeling is its mystery,
and the depth that can devour you in a single moment.


Love is the warm touch of their soul upon yours.


You know it is love when every song becomes about them.
When every sunlight carries a glimpse of their sparkling eyes.
When the only thing you crave is the warmth of their hand.
When the only sound in your head is their laugh.
When the only taste left on your tongue is their kiss.
When the only sun for you is their smile.
When every moment apart becomes an uncountable craving for their presence.
When the tiniest glimpse of them is enough to quiet every storm inside you.
When absence feels louder than any noise.
When every crowded room is empty until they arrive.
When their presence turns ordinary moments into memories before they are even gone.


Perhaps that is love: not something to be defined, but something to be felt until when words fail.
